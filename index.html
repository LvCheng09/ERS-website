Why maintain this journal?

Research evolves through implementations, measurements, failed ideas, and revised assumptions. This page keeps their status visible over time. Each training run should have a reproducible configuration, and each hypothesis should include a concrete test capable of disproving it.

02

Next experiments

Proposed experiments informed by the ERS architecture. Priorities and protocols remain editable; no benchmark outcome is claimed.

Control under matched budgets

Proposed

Compare flat and hierarchical Control while matching retrieval limits, lookahead depth, recovery attempts, and execution budgets.

Measure: accepted tasks, rejected unsupported outputs, candidate evaluations, executed Rules, total wall time, CPU and provider-token costs.

Compositional generalization

Proposed

Use held-out Event/Rule topologies to distinguish recombining existing Rules from memorizing familiar paths.

Measure: admissible novel-path success, justified refusals, failure causes, and performance with the Rule library frozen.

Dependency-aware revision

Proposed

Investigate maintaining multiple supports for a result and updating only the affected dependent closure after facts change.

Measure: correctness of retraction and retention, revalidated nodes, recomputation cost, and support provenance.

Learning & data selection

Exploratory

Explore whether failure categories, Rule coverage, and expected information gain could guide data selection or future Auto-Learn research.

Measure: learning benefit per annotation/training unit against random and fixed-curriculum baselines. Not implemented in v0.1.

03

Working hypotheses

These are speculative ideas for future experiments. Each should change status only when evidence is attached. A plausible interpretation is not a validated mechanism.

H1 · Type-aware Rule activation

Untested

A Rule-specific activation variable may benefit from different output semantics depending on Rule type: confidence, probability, intensity, or frequency, rather than one universal control quantity.

Test: compare calibrated selection and execution behavior under type-specific versus shared activation schemes on equivalent tasks.

H2 · Adaptive curriculum

Untested

Choosing observations based on current Rule coverage, uncertainty, and computational cost may improve the efficiency of future rule-learning or post-training.

Test: measure progress per sample or unit cost against random ordering and fixed curricula, with matched models and budgets.

H3 · Memory as search guidance

Untested

Reusing prior successful path patterns may speed repeated tasks if every path is rebound and readmitted using current facts, rather than copying earlier answers.

Test: compare exploration cost, accepted results, and false admissions with Memory guidance toggled on and off.

H4 · Task-dependent hierarchy

Untested

Adjusting Control depth to task complexity may outperform fixed-depth scheduling after accounting for coordination overhead.

Test: compare fixed and adaptive depths with identical candidate and execution budgets, reporting total cost rather than domain steps alone.

Ideas here describe possible research, not established neuroscience analogies, training results, or capabilities already released in the ERS demo.
